<b>Meizi Hanzi · 梅字</b> is a free and open-source app to learn to <b>write Chinese characters</b> stroke by stroke, with all 3,000 characters of the official <b>HSK 3.0</b> list.

<b>Write</b>
• Every stroke is checked as you write: shape, order and direction.
• Beginner mode with outline and hints, or expert mode from memory.
• Correct strokes snap, with a soft spring, into the exact brush shape.
• Spaced repetition (SM-2) brings each character back right before you forget it.
• Study by HSK level or by the 214 Kangxi radicals and their families.
• Watch animated stroke order, or draw a character you can't read to look it up.

<b>Listen and speak</b>
• Native speaker recordings (syllables and ~8,500 words) that work offline.
• Tone trainer, listening drills and pinyin typing.
• Vocabulary with spaced repetition: about 10,900 HSK words.
• Timed HSK mock exams and a placement test to find where to start.

<b>Read</b>
• Seven graded readers from HSK 1 to 7-9 with pinyin, translation and audio.
• Listen to each chapter with the current word highlighted, then answer comprehension questions.
• Add your own texts (TXT, EPUB or pasted text).

<b>Make it a habit</b>
• Daily goal, reminder, streak with a weekly freeze and achievements.
• Home screen widget and a shareable progress card.
• Statistics: calendar, accuracy, hardest characters and strokes, confused tones.

<b>Respects your phone and your privacy</b>
• No ads, no accounts and no internet permission: everything stays on your phone.
• "Ink" dark mode and 120 Hz only when needed, to save battery.
• Export and import your progress when you change phones.

English and Spanish interface, for phones and tablets. GPL-3.0 license.
